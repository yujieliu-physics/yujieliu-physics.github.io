Research

I am interested in information-theoretic and computational perspectives on quantum many-body physics.

Quantum many-body physics and information

Information-theoretic and computational perspectives on phases of matter, symmetries, topology, and nonequilibrium quantum systems.

Quantum simulation

Quantum simulation of exotic quantum matter and protocols for diagnosing many-body phases on quantum devices.

Open quantum systems

Dissipative phases of matter, quantum channels, and their connections to passive quantum error correction and robust information storage.

Quantum sensing

Fundamental limits and computational approaches to quantum sensing and spectroscopy.
