About

I love discussing physics! Reach out if you have thoughts.

My research broadly lies at the interface of quantum many-body physics and quantum information science, with recent interests in (a) Information-theoretic and computational perspective of quantum many-body physics. (b) Quantum simulation of exotic quantum matter. (c) Dissipative phases of matter in open quantum systems and their implication in passive quantum error correction. (d) Fundamental theory of quantum computational sensing and their practicality.
