About

I am a postdoctoral associate at the MIT Center for Theoretical Physics (CTP). I love discussing physics!

My research broadly lies at the interface of quantum many-body physics and quantum information science, with interests in quantum simulation, open quantum systems, quantum error correction, and quantum sensing.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the Technical University of Munich in 2024. Before that, I completed Part III of the Mathematical Tripos at the University of Cambridge and a BSc in Physics and Mathematical Sciences at the University of Melbourne.
